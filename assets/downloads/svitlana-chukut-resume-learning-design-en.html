Professional perspective

Prof. Svitlana Chukut is a learning designer, curriculum developer and public-administration scholar whose work connects disciplinary knowledge with professional decision-making. She combines more than 25 years in university teaching, research and academic leadership with Canadian postsecondary experience at George Brown Polytechnic. From June 2024 to April 2026, her work across curriculum-development and content-production roles contributed to 17 course initiatives and more than 50 modules. Her portfolio includes work-integrated learning, hospitality and tourism, construction, mass timber and nursing transition to practice. She develops learning outcomes, credible source collections, simulations, branching scenarios and authentic assessments, with Brightspace and H5P resources informed by accessibility expectations. Her distinctive contribution is the ability to connect research, curriculum structure and practical learning activities across disciplines, while working with academic leads, subject-matter experts and instructional designers.

Selected work and evidence

Construction decisions, made teachable

George Brown Polytechnic · module completed August 2024

In Estimating and Bidding in Construction, her portfolio documents an 80-page module with 52 resources. The learning sequence connects cost estimates, bid documents and evaluation criteria with H5P branching activities and a comprehensive bid-proposal assessment. It shows how she translates a professional process into decisions learners can practise and justify.

A research-informed teaching foundation

Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute · 2013–2025

At KPI, she developed bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral curricula and more than 10 online or hybrid courses in Moodle. Her teaching uses cases, projects, collaboration and research; her co-authored Fundamentals of Electronic Governance (2022) links conceptual knowledge to public-sector practice. This experience supports curriculum decisions beyond the production of individual learning objects.
