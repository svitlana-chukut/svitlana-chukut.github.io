Professional perspective

Prof. Svitlana Chukut is a senior academic leader whose experience combines departmental management, national higher-education standards, doctoral-program stewardship and Canadian curriculum development. At Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute, she served as Professor from 2013 to 2025, Head of Department from February 2015 to February 2021 and academic lead of the PhD program in Public Governance and Administration from 2018 to 2025. She chaired the Ministry of Education and Science’s specialist sub-commission from 2016 to 2019 and served as Deputy Chair of the relevant quality-assurance expert council in 2019–2020. Her work connects graduate competencies, curriculum architecture, research supervision and professional practice. With more than 60 defended master’s and doctoral/PhD dissertations supervised in total, she brings experience supporting both program-level development and individual scholarly trajectories. Her Canadian work adds practical insight into multidisciplinary digital course production and accessible learning.

Selected work and evidence

Standards developed through academic collaboration

Ministry of Education and Science of Ukraine · 2016–2019

As chair of Sub-commission 281, she worked with Ukrainian university colleagues to discuss, develop and refine standards in Public Governance and Administration at bachelor’s, master’s and doctoral levels. This involved competencies, learning outcomes and preparation for public-service work. Her later quality-assurance council role added experience in programme evaluation and accreditation.

Supervision connected with opportunities for students

KPI doctoral-program leadership · 2018–2025

Her supervision spans public communication, digital transformation, e-governance and related fields; 11 mentored student research projects received national competition prizes. She also initiated and organised the 2021 E-Governance Olympiad, with participation from 19 universities, connecting individual and team challenges with practitioner training and ministry internship opportunities.
