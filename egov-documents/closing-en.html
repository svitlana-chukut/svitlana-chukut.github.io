Priorities from the 2011 closing round table

Cabinet of Ministers Club, Kyiv · 20 May 2011

Source Academy draft programme (Ukrainian)

The following is a translation of the priority topics in the forum record and draft programme for the closing round table.

  • Review the proceedings and identify the first steps for implementing e-governance as part of Ukraine’s modernization.
  • Identify priority measures for access to public information and for innovative library projects supporting e-governance.
  • Identify priorities for implementing e-governance in central authorities and for advancing digital education.
  • Consider priority implementation measures from the perspective of a Microsoft Ukraine international IT expert.
  • Consider the draft legal instrument on priority measures for implementing e-governance and the draft strategy for e-governance development in the context of Ukraine’s modernization.

The programme set aside time to consider proposals from each of the preceding sessions. A contemporary report documents discussion of financing, the shortage of specialists and the need to make the benefits for communities better understood.
