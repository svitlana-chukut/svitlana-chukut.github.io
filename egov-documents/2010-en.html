Proposals for implementing e-governance in Ukraine

Information Policy Department, National Academy of Public Administration under the President of Ukraine · 2010

Source Departmental working proposals, with original institutions and target dates

Launch and regulatory measures

  1. Hold a joint round table on priority e-governance tasks to improve public understanding, with the Cabinet Secretariat, the Presidential Administration, heads of central executive authorities and heads of oblast state administrations. Invite the media and hold a closing press conference.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date September 2010
  2. Prepare a draft presidential decree on priority implementation measures. Require immediate action by the Cabinet, ministries, other central executive authorities, the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, oblast, Kyiv and Sevastopol city and district state administrations, and local governments. The Cabinet should ensure immediate action and prepare the required regulations within one month.
    Proposed leads Presidential Administration · Cabinet of Ministers · Ministries and other central executive authorities
    Target date September 2010
  3. Prepare a draft Cabinet resolution approving the plan for priority implementation measures. Include the measures below.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date October 2010

Measures for the draft action plan under item 3

  1. Implement the Electronic Ukraine and Electronic Government systems.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection · Ministries and other central executive authorities · Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, oblast, Kyiv and Sevastopol city and district state administrations, and local governments · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date October 2010–July 2011
  2. Amend the National Informatization Programme and adapt it to the requirements of both systems.
    Proposed leads State Committee for Informatization
    Target date October 2010
  3. Establish a dedicated E-Governance Development Directorate in the Cabinet Secretariat to organize and coordinate implementation.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat
    Target date October 2010
  4. Create a Ministry of Information Development based on the State Committee for Informatization, the State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection and the State Committee for Television and Radio Broadcasting. Make e-governance development and implementation one of its principal responsibilities.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection · State Committee for Television and Radio Broadcasting · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date November 2010
  5. Establish implementation units in ministries, other central executive authorities, the Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, and oblast, Kyiv and Sevastopol city and district state administrations. Have them report to the deputy head responsible for e-governance and recommend similar units in local governments.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · Ministries and other central executive authorities · Council of Ministers of the Autonomous Republic of Crimea, oblast, Kyiv and Sevastopol city and district state administrations, and local governments · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date January 2011
  6. Set up an Institute of E-Governance within the Academy to support implementation through teaching, methodology and research.
    Proposed leads National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date December 2010
  7. Prepare and obtain authorization for one-month full-time or three-month part-time professional development courses for civil servants.
    Proposed leads Ministry of Education and Science · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date January 2011
  8. Deliver comprehensive training on e-governance implementation and development. Require 120 classroom hours in either course format and award a professional development diploma on completion.
    Proposed leads National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date November–December, February–March, March–April and May–June (2011 as stated in the source)
  9. Develop teaching and methodology resources and conduct research on e-governance implementation and development.
    Proposed leads National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date June 2011
  10. Add “manager of e-governance organizational systems” and “specialist in e-governance organizational systems” to the Ukrainian Classification of Occupations DK 003:2005. Align the roles with public administration and ICT labour market needs and support both national systems.
    Proposed leads Ministry of Labour and Social Policy · Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date February 2011
  11. Prepare and obtain accreditation for the master’s programme in speciality 8.150110 E-Governance, field 1501 Public Administration.
    Proposed leads Ministry of Education and Science · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date January 2011
  12. Prepare and obtain authorization for a bachelor’s programme in Communications and Services, field 1501 Public Administration.
    Proposed leads Ministry of Education and Science · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date February 2011
  13. Commission targeted government-funded places for bachelor’s and master’s training in e-governance.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · Ministry of Economy · Ministry of Education and Science · Ministry of Information Development · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date May–June 2011
  14. Prepare a specialized E-Governance library collection endorsed by the Ministry of Education and Science.
    Proposed leads National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department · Ministry of Education and Science
    Target date November 2010–February 2011
  15. Earmark state funding to implement and operate both national systems, including funds for civil-service training.
    Proposed leads Ministry of Finance · Ministry of Economy · Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date 2010–2011

Further draft Cabinet resolutions

  1. Draft a Cabinet resolution approving a framework for training, retraining and professional development of civil servants in e-governance.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date April–May 2011
  2. Draft a Cabinet resolution approving admission procedures for the professional master’s programme in speciality 8.150110 E-Governance in Public Administration and employment arrangements for graduates.
    Proposed leads Information Resources and Technology Development Directorate of the Cabinet Secretariat · State Committee for Informatization · National Academy of Public Administration, Information Policy Department
    Target date March 2011
