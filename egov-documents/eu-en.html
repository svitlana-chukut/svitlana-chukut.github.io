Cooperation programme with the European Union

Information Policy Department, National Academy of Public Administration under the President of Ukraine

Source Academy departmental cooperation programme

  1. The Academy is Ukraine’s principal teaching and research institution for training highly qualified e-governance specialists through its master’s programme in E-Governance within Public Administration.
  2. Cooperate on the E-Governance master’s programme.
  • Arrange placements abroad for master’s students and invite foreign experts to teach in the programme.
  • Exchange faculty, experience, research and practical results. Share information on research, teaching and methodology for the programme.
  • Exchange curricula and experience with modern teaching methods. Equip dedicated classrooms for E-Governance and Mobile Governance and Communications.
  • Disseminate information and research findings and exchange topics of common interest, including through articles in the international scholarly journal E-Governance (www.egov.in.ua, address cited in the original).
  1. Develop a bachelor’s programme in Communications and Services.
  • Issue joint bachelor’s degrees, organize placements abroad and invite foreign experts to teach.
  • Exchange faculty, experience, research, practical results, curricula and modern teaching methods.
  • Equip dedicated classrooms for E-Governance and Mobile Governance and Communications.
  1. Invite international experts and leading e-governance specialists to take part in E-Governance Days.
  2. Provide comprehensive professional development for civil servants and local government officials through one-month courses away from work or three-month courses taken alongside work.
  3. Introduce advanced digital education technologies and apply lessons from European schools, especially on internet access for pupils.
  4. Seek international technical assistance to develop high-quality digital education in rural areas.
  5. Turn schools, particularly rural schools, into public internet access points.
  6. Launch a national project to implement and develop e-governance in Ukraine.
  7. Draft a national e-governance development strategy and invite European Union specialists to join the drafting group.
  8. Cooperate on protecting personal data as e-governance is introduced.
  9. Monitor digital readiness at the central, regional and local levels.
  10. Seek EU technical assistance for pilot projects on digital regions and cities, digital public services at each tier of government, sectoral e-governance such as agriculture, health and social affairs, and e-democracy.
